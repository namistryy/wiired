Who We Are

Design that works as hard as you do.

WIIRED Studio is a Seattle-based UI/UX design studio founded by two computer science students who believe great design is never just surface-level — it's structure, story, and strategy working together.

We partner with startups and small businesses to transform complex ideas into clear, beautiful, and functional digital experiences.

How We Work

Our process

Six steps from first brief to final retrospective.
01
Discovery
Audit the experience, map user flows, identify pain points, and define the real problem.
02
Wireframing
Low and mid-fidelity frames to map structure and hierarchy before touching aesthetics.
03
Redesign
High-fidelity designs — updated palette, type, assets, and interactive UI elements.
04
Implementation
Approved designs become real, working products — responsive, functional, and deployed.
05
Deployment
Launch, configure, and make everything live, stable, and ready for real users.
06
Retrospective
Review what worked, what didn't, and how to iterate. Great design is a continuous loop.
